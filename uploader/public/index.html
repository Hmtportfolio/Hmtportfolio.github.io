How to change credentials or bucket?

To change your Cloudflare R2 credentials, open uploader/.env in your code editor and update CF_ACCOUNT_ID, CF_ACCESS_KEY_ID, CF_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY, or CF_BUCKET_NAME, then restart the uploader.
